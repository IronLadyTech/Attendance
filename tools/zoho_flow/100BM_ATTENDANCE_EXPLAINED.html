100BM Attendance — Explained

Plain-language guide for RM / sales / ops: how 100BM (FastTrack) attendance is tracked and what appears in CRM.

Overview

When a 100BM FastTrack session runs on Zoom, the system automatically updates attendance in Zoho CRM for invited leads. It checks at two fixed times:

CheckpointWhenPurpose
First check15 minutes inMark who is in the room; flag missing leads as No so sales can call
Final check30 minutes inFinal Yes / No / Absent for everyone

Nothing is marked at join time. CRM updates only at 15 min and 30 min.

Which leads are processed?

A lead is included when its FT Invite Date matches the session date. No payment status is required.

CRM fields used

FieldWhat goes in it
FT AttendanceYes, No, or Absent
FT Attended DateThe session date — filled in whenever FT Attendance becomes Yes
FT Invite DateUsed only to decide who is part of today's session (not changed)

The three attendance values in CRM

ValueMeaning
YesIn the Zoom room at the 30-minute check
NoNot in the room at 15 min, or joined but left before 30 min
AbsentInvited lead who never joined Zoom (confirmed at 30 min)

Important for sales: No at 15 min does not mean absent. It means “not in the room right now” — they may still join before 30 min and become Yes.

Simple rule for one person

  1. 15 min — In Zoom? → Yes. Not in Zoom? → No.
  2. 30 min — In Zoom? → Yes. Joined but left? → No. Never joined? → Absent.

What RM / sales should do

  1. At 15 min — Filter CRM for No. Call them — they can still join before 30 min.
  2. At 30 min — Attendance is final:
    • Yes = attended
    • No = joined but dropped / left early
    • Absent = never showed on Zoom

How the system works (logic only)

At session start

  • Zoom session begins.
  • The system starts tracking who joins, who is still in the room, and who has left.
  • No CRM updates yet.

At 15 minutes (first check)

Step 1 — Individual updates

WhoWhat CRM gets
Currently in ZoomYes (+ FT Attended Date)
Joined earlier but left before 15 minNo (via batch sweep below)
Not in Zoom yet (invited lead)No (via batch sweep below)

Step 2 — Batch sweep (all invited leads for that day)

  • Any lead whose FT Attendance is still blank → marked No
  • This covers people who never joined and people who joined and left early

Guests not in CRM: If someone joined Zoom but their email is not in CRM, they are logged to an internal sheet for manual follow-up. CRM leads always get No or Yes — not left blank.

At 30 minutes (final check)

Step 1 — Individual updates

WhoWhat CRM gets
Currently in ZoomYes (can upgrade No → Yes for late joiners; FT Attended Date set)
Joined at some point but left before 30 minNo (can downgrade Yes → No for dropouts)

Step 2 — Batch safety net (all invited leads for that day)

Join historyFinal value
In Zoom at 30 minYes
Joined Zoom at some point but not in room at 30 minNo
Never joined Zoom at allAbsent

Also: No → Yes if they are in the room at 30 min (late joiner). No → Absent if they never joined Zoom at all.

Rules the system follows

RuleBehaviour
Already Yes at 30 min, still in roomStays Yes
No at 15 min, joins and stays till 30 minUpgraded to Yes
Yes at 15 min, leaves before 30 minDowngraded to No
No at 15 min, never joinsUpgraded to Absent at 30 min
Invited lead after 30 minNever left blank — always Yes, No, or Absent

One-line summary

15 min: In room = Yes; not in room = No (call them).
30 min: In room = Yes; joined but left = No; never joined = Absent.
FT Attended Date is filled with the session date whenever attendance is Yes.
