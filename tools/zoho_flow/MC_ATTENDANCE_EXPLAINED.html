MC Attendance — Explained

Overview

When an MC Zoom session runs, the system automatically updates attendance in Zoho CRM for paid batch leads. It checks at three fixed times:

CheckpointWhenPurpose
First check15 minutes inMark who is in the room; flag missing leads as No so sales can call
Final check30 minutes inSet Yes / No / Absent for everyone
Hour check60 minutes inLate opportunity: if still in Zoom and was No or Absent, upgrade to Yes

Nothing is marked at join time. CRM updates only at 15 min, 30 min, and 60 min.

The three attendance values in CRM

ValueMeaning
YesIn the Zoom room at the 30-minute check, or upgraded at the 60-minute check after joining late
NoNot in the room at 15 min, or joined but left before 30 min (and not back in room at 60 min)
AbsentPaid batch lead who never joined Zoom by the 30-min check (can still become Yes if they join by 60 min)

Important for sales: No at 15 min does not mean absent. It means “not in the room right now” — they may still join before 30 min (or by 60 min) and become Yes.

Simple rule for one person

  1. 15 min — In Zoom? → Yes. Not in Zoom? → No.
  2. 30 min — In Zoom? → Yes. Joined but left? → No. Never joined? → Absent.
  3. 60 min — In Zoom and was No or Absent? → Yes. Already Yes? → stays Yes. Not in Zoom? → status unchanged.

What RM / sales should do

  1. At 15 min — Filter CRM for No. Call them — they can still join before 30 min (or by 60 min).
  2. At 30 min — Attendance is set to Yes / No / Absent for the batch.
  3. At 60 min — Late joiners who are in the room can still move from No or Absent to Yes.
  4. After Day 2 (30 min) — MC Completed is updated automatically for eligible leads (at the Day 2 final check).

How the system works (logic only)

At session start

  • Zoom session begins.
  • The system starts tracking who joins, who is still in the room, and who has left.
  • No CRM updates yet.

At 15 minutes (first check)

Step 1 — Individual updates

WhoWhat CRM gets
Currently in ZoomYes
Joined earlier but left before 15 minNo (via batch sweep below)
Not in Zoom yet (paid batch lead)No (via batch sweep below)

Step 2 — Batch sweep (all paid batch leads for that day)

  • Any lead whose attendance is still blank → marked No
  • This covers people who never joined and people who joined and left early

Guests not in CRM: If someone joined Zoom but their email is not in CRM, they are logged to an internal sheet for manual follow-up. CRM leads always get No or Yes — not left blank.

At 30 minutes (final check)

Step 1 — Individual updates

WhoWhat CRM gets
Currently in ZoomYes (can upgrade No → Yes for late joiners)
Joined at some point but left before 30 minNo (can downgrade Yes → No for dropouts)

Step 2 — Batch safety net (all paid batch leads for that day)

Join historyValue
In Zoom at 30 minYes
Joined Zoom at some point but not in room at 30 minNo
Never joined Zoom at allAbsent

Step 3 — Day 2 only: After the 30-minute check on Day 2, eligible leads are marked MC Completed in CRM.

At 60 minutes (hour check — late opportunity)

Step 1 — Individual updates

WhoWhat CRM gets
Currently in ZoomYes (via mark_yes; also upgrades No / Absent)

Step 2 — Batch hour sweep

SituationResult
In Zoom at 60 min + was No or AbsentUpgraded to Yes
Already YesStays Yes
Not in Zoom at 60 minUnchanged (No stays No, Absent stays Absent)

The hour check does not mark new Absents or Nos. It only gives a second chance for people who are present.

Rules the system follows

RuleBehaviour

Yes at 15 min, leaves before 30 minDowngraded to No

One-line summary

15 min: In room = Yes; not in room = No (call them).
30 min: In room = Yes; joined but left = No; never joined = Absent.
60 min: In room + No/Absent → Yes; not in room → status unchanged.
